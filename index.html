☼ the lore · wiki

PROVIDENTIA DUCE

Ninefold Industries is the last surviving piece of Lucifer’s rule on Earth. To the public, it was a media company, remembered for strange late-night television and experimental broadcasts. In reality, Ninefold is the earthly counterpart to the Netherworld, built to maintain balance between the seven sins, the seven virtues, and the nine circles. Every signal, every broadcast, every transmission served a greater purpose. Ninefold was never created to entertain. It was built to keep the barrier between worlds intact.

first known broadcast, ch.09 archive
MIAMI

Miami is a coastal city in southeastern Florida, United States. While most know it for its beaches, nightlife, and Art Deco architecture, others recognize it for something far stranger. Hidden beneath the city lies the strongest connection between Earth and the Netherworld. Since the late twentieth century, Miami has also served as the headquarters of Ninefold Industries, the last remnant of Lucifer’s influence on Earth.

crossroads of shadows, coastal nexus
LATE NIGHT WITH MR. INFERNO

One of Ninefold Industries’ most infamous projects was Inferno Studios, the company behind the mysterious television program Late Night with Mr. Inferno. The show aired sporadically around Miami during the late 1980s and early 1990s, gaining a devoted following among insomniacs, conspiracy theorists, and anyone drawn to the city’s strange reputation.

inferno studios, ch.09, circa 1989
FALL OF MAN

Fall of Man (Latin: Lapsus hominis) is a research committee at Miami University dedicated to investigating the city’s unexplained history and its long-standing connection to supernatural activity. Made up mostly of students, professors, and independent researchers, the group exists somewhere between legitimate academic study and urban legend.
